#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Mobile Bottom Chrome Overlap**: Floating audio pill and back-to-top button compete with the mobile bottom navigation bar in the thumb zone on small screens.
  - *Fix*: Tuck the ambient audio player into the navigation drawer on mobile viewports (<640px).
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable adapt components/mobile-bottom-nav.tsx`
- **[P2] Mechanical Animation Easing**: Tailwind `animate-bounce` on cart badge and hero hearts creates jittery movement.
  - *Fix*: Replace bounce with smooth exponential ease-out pulse transitions.
  - *Suggested command*: `/impeccable animate app/globals.css`
- **[P3] Storybook Direct Page Index**: Navigating through multi-spread chapters requires sequential flipping.
  - *Fix*: Add a table-of-contents quick-jump dropdown from the ribbon bookmark.
